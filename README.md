## Health Care
Health Care is a comprehensive healthcare management template designed to facilitate online appointment booking and patient management for clinics and hospitals. It offers a user-friendly platform that connects patients with healthcare providers, streamlining the process of scheduling medical consultations

Key Features:
> Appointment Scheduling
 - Patients can easily book, reschedule, or cancel appointments online, providing flexibility and convenience.
> Patient Management
 - Healthcare providers have access to a centralized database of patient records, including medical history and consultation notes, enhancing the quality of care.
> Doctor Availability
 - Doctors can set and manage their availability, allowing patients to view open slots and book appointments accordingly.
> Admin and User Dashboards
 - The template includes dedicated dashboards for clinic administrators, doctors, and patients, facilitating efficient management and navigation.
